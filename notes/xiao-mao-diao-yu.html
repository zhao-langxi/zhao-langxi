小猫钓鱼

小猫钓鱼 is a childhood story a lot of Mandarin speakers already know: the little cat goes fishing, gets distracted, does not stay on the task. It is also, literally, how I spend time on the water. Both readings are intended. The idiom is about attention. The photographs are about a rod, a boat, and a fish that either comes up or does not.

This site is the essay door. Shipped code lives on jadexzhao. The catch photograph stays on About. The frames below are the rest of the same fishing set, full photographs rather than square crops.

Jade Zhao (赵郎溪) / zhao-langxi

In Chinese the family name comes first. 赵 is Zhao. 郎溪 is Langxi, the given name I use in public. Written together: 赵郎溪. In English the order flips: Jade Zhao. Same person. Two conventions, not two identities.

The handle on this door is zhao-langxi because this room is the Chinese public name, romanised and hyphenated so it can live in a URL. jadexzhao is the other public GitHub face, where the builds sit. I do not merge the two sites. Readers who want proof of work should follow the link; readers who want the thinking can stay here.

she/her

I put she/her next to the name because mixed English and Chinese, plus romanisation, get misgendered more often than people admit in a hallway. A short pronoun line is cheaper than a correction later. It is a clarification, not a speech. I am not going to argue the whole internet from an IU-adjacent notes page. I am going to label the person the page is about.

Reformed iPad kid

I grew up with screens. That is the honest version of “reformed iPad kid”: not a TED story, not a morality play about one device. I learned early that a screen can hurt you and that it can also be a place you build something back. The origin essay on About already covers the internet that turned on me. This note does not invent extra childhood scenes.

What I do with that now is quieter. I would rather maintain systems for people who get left outside of tech, including kids who meet a form, a portal, or a classroom tool that assumes they already belong, than pretend the stack is neutral because it is digital. Screens are not the enemy. Unexamined defaults are.

How AI actually works, here

In my framing, AI is not magic and it is not a person. It is a system with handoffs, training data, interface copy, and a long list of assumptions about who the user already is. Capability growth does not automatically mean inclusion. If the form already leaves a newcomer stuck, wrapping a model around it does not fix the sequencing. It just makes the failure harder to see.

That is why this door talks about systems friction and why the AI and law pointer stays a pointer: lease notes and public-service ethics notes, not a treatise, not legal advice, not an IU research product. I care about who can complete the task after the demo, who can maintain the page after the student team leaves, and who gets blamed when the error message says “invalid.”

Drafting on these notes was AI-assisted. Accountability remains with Jade Zhao. I do not put tool brand names on this site. If a sentence is wrong, it is my wrong sentence.
