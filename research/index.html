Research and methods

Research hub

Each file below supports the essays. Open the one that matches what you need: reading lists, citation rules, or draft review notes.

  • Evidence labels

    Read this first if you are new. It defines Observed, Reported, Interpreted, and Open question on every essay.

  • Open bibliography

    Cited and candidate sources with stable links. Use when you want to check a claim or suggest a reference via GitHub.

  • HRI reading library

    Papers, tools, and open robotics projects grouped for essay research ... not a course syllabus.

  • Essay reviews

    Editorial notes on existing drafts (argument, repetition, HRI bridge). No silent overwrites of essay HTML.

  • Flagship essay plan

    Working outline for The right to interrupt ... interruption, accessibility, and authority in shared spaces.

  • Research notes folder

    Scratch notes and reading logs that may feed future essays. Informal; not published as finished work.

Contributing references

Use GitHub Issues (template: research reference) or a pull request that adds a row to bibliography.md with a stable link. Do not paste paywalled full text without permission.
