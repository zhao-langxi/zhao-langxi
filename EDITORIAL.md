## What this is

An independent publication exploring **robotics, human-robot interaction (HRI), and what happens when intelligent machines become part of everyday life**.

Not a robotics engineering blog. Not a résumé. A traceable writing and research project: question → evidence → interpretation → what remains unknown.

## Guiding question

> We keep asking what robots will be capable of doing. What should we be asking about the people who will have to live alongside them?

## Evidence labels (use in every essay)

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Observed** | Something you personally witnessed or documented. |
| **Reported** | A claim supported by a linked source. |
| **Interpreted** | Your analysis. |
| **Open question** | Not established yet. |

## Research recipe (each essay)

1. One **documented** robot, programme, or deployment (named, linkable).  
2. One **cited** HRI or adjacent source (paraphrased, not overstated).  
3. One **original question** of yours.  
4. End on an **open question**, not generic futurism.
