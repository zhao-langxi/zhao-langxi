## Who is looking

I look at rooms the way I look at water: wait, then notice. This door is a personal journal, not a studio and not a list of jobs. Photography here is a habit. Film when I remember the camera. A phone when the light is already happening. BeReal days because those are pictures I actually took.

The Instagram is [@zhao.langxi](https://www.instagram.com/zhao.langxi). The bio is the original story: *xiao mao diao yu* ... little cat fishing ... *pov reformed ipad kid*.

## The name

赵郎溪 is how this door is written. In pinyin that is Zhào Lángxī ... ordinary dictionary readings. Jade Zhao is the English name I use.

Why those two given-name characters were chosen, and any family story behind them, is still waiting for me to write it in my own words. I am not filling that gap with a pretty guess.

## Where the days come from

Chinese American. I grew up in a family Chinese restaurant in central Indiana (Greenfield, Indiana). I study Informatics at Indiana University in Bloomington, expected May 2027. Spring 2026 I lived a semester at Universidad Complutense de Madrid. I speak English, Fuzhounese, and Spanish.

When I am not walking somewhere: film photography, gardening, reading, matcha, a duck-farm throughline that is culture, not a product.
