Where Digital Humans are being built

Looking human is not the product. The question is still trust on an ordinary day: when a system looks and talks like a person, what makes people use it, believe the disclosure, and keep using it for real work. This note maps public places where that stack shows up. It foreshadows study, not a move or a launch.

Jade Zhao · work in progress · last updated September 2026

China

China has a real 数字人 industry. Named platforms include Baidu’s digital-employee / 曦灵 stack, Huawei Cloud MetaStudio, and Xiaoice in Beijing. Industry writeups also point to Shenzhen, Beijing, Shanghai, and Hangzhou as hubs. What those products emphasise in public docs is often video, livestream anchors, brand faces, and service-hall Q&A bolted to a knowledge base or large language model ... not deep work on trust, disclosure, and anthropomorphism as research questions. For a US student the practical catch is Mandarin-first hiring and networks, China’s personal-information rules and generative-AI labelling expectations, and US–China AI friction that complicates cross-border collaboration. Worth watching as a large product market. Not an obvious next place for the ordinary-day trust question.

Places closer to useful business interfaces

These hubs and company types sit nearer to conversational business interfaces ... customer experience, training, and digital workers that have to survive contact with a real workflow.

  • London · Synthesia ... enterprise AI video moving into interactive avatars for sales, support, and trainers. Source
  • San Francisco and Auckland · Soul Machines ... Digital Workforce for CX, HR, sales, and related face-to-face workflows; public materials put trust and engagement in the product language. Source
  • New Zealand / US multi-hub · UneeQ ... real-time digital humans for customer experience and immersive training. Source
  • Tel Aviv · D-ID ... photoreal digital humans and real-time visual agents for CX, learning, and communication. Source
  • Seoul · DeepBrain AI ... conversational AI humans with a public retail CX case (Hyundai Premium Outlet Songdo). Source

Two company types to watch alongside cities: real-time conversational digital-human platforms for CX and employee training, and enterprise interactive-avatar APIs that sit on video or 3D stacks.
