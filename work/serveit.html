ServeIT

IU Luddy nonprofit tech clinic (Bloomington, Indiana). Partners without a developer on call. Accessibility from the first chunk paste, not a late QA pass.

I work as Website Team Lead at ServeIT Clinic: WCAG 2.1 baselines, Cascade WCMS + IU Rivet composition, and handoffs staff can maintain.

The restaurant-floor lesson still applies: a system either survives contact with a real visitor or it doesn’t. Clinic work is where that instinct meets Bloomington (Indiana) partners.
