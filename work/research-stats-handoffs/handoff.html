5. IA / screen map

A. Theme filters
What: Five tabs (Digital Humans · Privacy · UX · Nonprofit · Restaurant).
Why: One theme at a time; footer throughline follows the tab.
What to build: Keep tabs = themes; default Digital Humans.
B. Stat cards
What: Big number + claim; category; What / Why / What to build; role link.
Why: Forces every market figure into shipping language.
What to build: Never ship a card with only a percentage.
C. Multi-select
What: Select up to 3 cards; count shows n / 3; persists in localStorage.
Why: Clustering = pick a next project from evidence.
What to build: Cap stays at 3.
D. Cluster stepper + lock
What: Appears when 2 to 3 selected cards span themes; Previous / Next / Lock.
Why: Cross-theme picks need an explicit next-build idea.
What to build: Theme project ideas already in JS; Lock persists.
E. Session notes
What: Textarea; auto-save to localStorage.
Why: Capture this week’s ship intent next to the lock.
What to build: Keep local-only.

Happy path: open board → Digital Humans → select 2 to 3 cards → stepper if multi-theme → Lock → notes → build elsewhere.
