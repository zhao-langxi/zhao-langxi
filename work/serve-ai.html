Serve-AI

Responsible adoption for community organizations ... accessibility alongside automation. Founding note from Jade; project materials are shared.

Serve-AI is ServeIT Clinic’s public-interest AI track (PIT-UN). The goal is practical: help partners adopt generative tools without breaking trust or accessibility.
