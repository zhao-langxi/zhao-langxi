How I got here

The UE desk was never about a prettier policy. It was whether someone could tell what was collected, why, where it went, and what happened if they said no. Market research is published context only ... not personal metrics. Related board: research stats · privacy. Portfolio copy: jadexzhao/plain-disclosure.
