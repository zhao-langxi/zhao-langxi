Privacy worksheet

Plain Disclosure

Most people skip the terms. This worksheet makes a privacy blurb answer four questions before Agree: what they take, why, where it goes, and what happens if you say no.

About 91% of people consent without reading (Deloitte, 2017). Even after reading, about 55% still fail to comprehend the privacy content (IEEE Access, 2021).
