About this publication

Jade Zhao · 赵郎溪

Who writes

Jade Zhao. Informatics student at Indiana University. I write from human-centred design and technology, not as a robotics engineer. 赵郎溪 is an independent essay publication, not Indiana University research and not peer-reviewed work.

Why these questions

Intelligent systems are entering shared life: screens that speak like people, tools that filter choices, institutions that catalogue “digital humans.” This publication asks what that means for trust, interruption, accessibility, and agency ... especially after the demo ends.

How evidence is handled

Essays use four labels: Observed, Reported, Interpreted, and Open question. I do not claim fieldwork I did not do. Public catalogues and media coverage are evidence of what institutions publish, not proof that people trust or use a system well.
