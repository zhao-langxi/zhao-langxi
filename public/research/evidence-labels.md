# Evidence labels

Use in every essay and research note.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Observed** | Something you personally witnessed or documented. |
| **Reported** | A claim supported by a linked source. |
| **Interpreted** | Your analysis. |
| **Open question** | Not established yet. |

Example inline: `[Reported] Smart LAB listing describes …` · `[Interpreted] That suggests …` · `[Open question] Whether users return …`
