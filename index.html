Open publication · human-robot interaction

赵郎溪 · zhao-langxi

An independent, open-source publication exploring robotics, human-robot interaction, and what happens when intelligent machines become part of everyday life ... by Jade Zhao. Not peer-reviewed; source and licences on GitHub.

We keep asking what robots will be capable of doing. What should we be asking about the people who will have to live alongside them?

I write from human-centred design and technology, not as a robotics engineer. Perspective on people, institutions, and trust is enough for this door. Earlier essays on Digital Humans and screen-bound systems stay here as the starting point before bodies and routes enter the story.

jadexzhao (ships) · matchaxmoxie (coursework) · @zhao.langxi (photos, secondary)

Three themes

Future pieces and revised drafts organise under these headings

  • Human First

    Consent, trust, accessibility, interruption, responsibility, and the boundaries of automation.

  • Robots in the Wild

    Robots in museums, hospitals, workplaces, and public space ... using published evidence, not implied fieldwork.

  • After the Demo

    What a demonstration proves, what it hides, and what would need to happen for everyday reliability.

Evidence labels for every essay: Observed · Reported · Interpreted · Open question. See research/evidence-labels.md.
