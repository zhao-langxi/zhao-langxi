Digital Humans as business interfaces

I lead Digital Humans work at Luddy LINK: AI-driven virtual personas for real workflows. Core question: when an AI system looks and communicates like a person, what makes people trust it, use it, and keep using it? Case study: Digital Humans. Market-context board: research stats. Profile: about. Shipped code: jadexzhao.

Published market context: 72% of businesses use generative AI, up from 37% in 2023 (McKinsey, 2025); only 5% of enterprises integrate AI at scale (MIT, 2025) ... a 67 pp adoption-to-scale gap on those inputs; 51% report negative AI consequences, led by inaccuracy (Master of Code, 2026). Supporting: 13.5% of EU enterprises used AI in 2024 (Eurostat); 85% of customer service leaders plan conversational generative AI pilots in 2025 (Gartner); half of US adults feel more concerned than excited about AI in daily life (Pew).
