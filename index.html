Digital Humans as business interfaces

Jade Zhao · 赵郎溪 · Digital Humans Project Lead at Luddy LINK · Informatics (Business and Society) @ Indiana University · expected May 2027

When an AI system looks and talks like a person, people decide quickly whether to trust it, use it, and come back. I lead Digital Humans work at Luddy LINK, which places students on applied technology projects at IU Luddy. The question on this door is not how human the demo looks. It is whether a person-shaped interface earns a place in a real workflow.

This is the writing room. Hire-facing narrative lives on IU Pages. Shipped code lives on jadexzhao. Campus classroom lives on matchaxmoxie. Working files for this door live in the zhao-langxi GitHub organisation.

Field note

McKinsey’s early 2024 survey found that 72% of organisations use AI in at least one business function (McKinsey, 2024). MIT NANDA’s 2025 report found that only about 5% of custom enterprise AI tools reach production (MIT NANDA, 2025). Those are two published inputs, not one survey, and not a personal metric. What that gap means for person-like interfaces is the Digital Humans case.
