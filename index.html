Digital Humans as business interfaces

I lead Digital Humans work at Luddy LINK, which places students on applied technology projects at IU Luddy. The work is about AI-driven virtual personas in real business workflows, not avatar demos for their own sake. When an AI system looks and talks like a person, what makes people trust it, use it, and keep using it? Handshake influencer narrative lives on IU Pages ... then Handshake content creator, same question.

Digital Humans case study

Why now

McKinsey’s early 2024 survey found that 72% of organisations use AI in at least one business function (McKinsey, 2024). MIT NANDA’s 2025 report found that only about 5% of custom enterprise AI tools reach production (MIT NANDA, 2025). Those are two published inputs, not one survey ... and not a personal metric. What that gap means for person-like interfaces: Digital Humans case. Full board under Research.
