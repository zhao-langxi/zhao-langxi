Independent publication · human-robot interaction

What changes when intelligent machines share our spaces?

We keep asking what robots will be capable of doing. What should we be asking about the people who will have to live alongside them?

I write from human-centred design and technology, not as a robotics engineer. Essays use evidence labels so readers can see what is observed, reported, or interpreted.

Featured essay · in progress

Human First · planned

The right to interrupt

Human control should mean more than an emergency stop. When a robot enters a workplace, museum or shared public space, who gets to tell it to stop? What happens when a person cannot move out of its way, does not understand its intentions, or needs human assistance?

Outline and reading list: editorial/flagship-plan.md. The essay will publish here when drafted and reviewed.
