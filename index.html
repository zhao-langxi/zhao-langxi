Notes on systems that leave people outside

I write here when I want the thinking on the page, not only the shipped site. My focus is the work that keeps systems usable after the visible part is finished: reliable handoffs, maintainable builds for partners without in-house engineering support, and clear answers when a public form or clinic handoff assumes you already belong. I lead web work at Serve IT and coordinate the Mentor Apprenticeship Programme (MAP) within Faculty and Staff for Student Excellence (FASE), including as STEM (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics) MAP Coordinator. Growing up in a family restaurant shaped that standard. After graduation I am looking toward software engineering roles in databases, internal systems, IT, and ETL. The fishing photographs are mine. The GitHub bio is 小猫钓鱼, a children’s story about staying with the rod. GitHub, LinkedIn, and Instagram all use zhao-langxi. That is one name, not a rebrand. Code lives on jadexzhao. The classroom lives on matchaxmoxie.
