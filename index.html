Digital Humans as useful business interfaces

Looking human is not the product. Digital Humans earn a place when they help people on an ordinary day ... including for small organisations that cannot afford a face that fails on the floor. There is no single right way a useful system has to look. Trust is built in disclosure, handoff, and the Tuesday question, not the demo. Show up for the people who actually use it. You are not behind if the path changed; be here for this version of the work, not the perfect one in a pitch deck.

Field note

McKinsey’s early 2024 survey found that 72% of organisations use AI in at least one business function (McKinsey, 2024). MIT NANDA’s 2025 report found that only about 5% of custom enterprise AI tools reach production (MIT NANDA, 2025). Those are two published inputs, not one survey, and not a personal metric. What that gap means for person-like interfaces is the Digital Humans case.
