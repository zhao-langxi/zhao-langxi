Digital Humans as business interfaces

I lead Digital Humans work at Luddy LINK: AI-driven virtual personas for real workflows, not avatar demos for their own sake. Core question: when an AI system looks and communicates like a person, what makes people trust it, use it, and keep using it?

Digital Humans case study About

Why this work matters now

Published market figures (not personal metrics) put the question in context: 72% of businesses use generative AI (McKinsey, 2025), yet only 5% integrate AI at scale (MIT, 2025), and trust is already under pressure (51% report negative consequences, led by inaccuracy (Master of Code, 2026)). Digital Humans work sits where adoption and trust meet.
